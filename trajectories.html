How it works

  1. 1Watch

    Play the clip. Every time in the trajectory is a button that jumps there.

  2. 2Read the steps

    Each step shows a thought, the tool call and its output. The final answer is already verified.

  3. 3Rate each thought

    ✓ correct, ⚑ flag or ✗ incorrect. Add a note when something is off.

  4. 4Save & export

    “Mark the other N correct” fills in the rest. Export when finished and send the file.
