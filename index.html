CAT-Agent
Clinician question review

Open your review batch

Drag the batch folder you received (for example batch_001) onto this box, or choose it below. Each video comes with its GPT-generated questions.

Your files stay on this computer. Nothing is uploaded; progress is saved in this browser.

How it works

  1. 1Watch

    Play the surgery video. Time chips jump to each moment.

  2. 2Mark the best

    Star the best one or two of the three questions.

  3. 3Check the answer

    Correct, partly correct, incorrect or can't tell.

  4. 4Edit if needed

    Fix the question, options or answer. The original is kept.

  5. 5Export

    Click Done & next for each video, then export and send the file.

CAT-Agent is a research project at Johns Hopkins University.
