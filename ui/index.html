CAT-Agent
Clinician question review · Johns Hopkins University

Open your review batch

Drag the batch folder you received (for example batch_001) onto this box, or choose it below. Each video comes with its GPT-generated questions.

Enter your name or ID to start. Nothing is saved without it.

Your files stay on this computer; nothing is uploaded. Progress saves automatically in this browser and, in Chrome or Edge, inside your batch folder, so you can stop and continue later. Use Save progress any time. Your active time per video is recorded in your progress and export files.

How it works

  1. 1Watch

    Play the surgery video. Time chips jump to each moment.

  2. 2Mark the best

    Star the best one or two of the three questions.

  3. 3Check the answer

    Correct, partly correct, incorrect or can't tell.

  4. 4Edit if needed

    Edit the question or the answer. The GPT original is kept next to your edit.

  5. 5Save & export

    Save progress any time and continue later. When finished, export and send the file.
